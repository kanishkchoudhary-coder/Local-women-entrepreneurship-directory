WHY HERLOCAL?

Every local story deserves to be discovered.

Talented entrepreneurs don't always have the visibility they deserve. HerLocal imagines a simpler way to find women-led businesses, understand their offerings and connect directly.

This is a student prototype. Current listings are examples for learning and testing. Real submissions, verification and customer reviews are planned for later development.
